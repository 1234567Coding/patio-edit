Affiliate & Advertising Disclosure

By The Patio Edit Team September 27, 2026

This page explains, in plain English, how The Patio Edit makes money — because a site built on telling you to buy furniture once owes you total honesty about the times it suggests you buy something.

What affiliate links are

Some links on this site are affiliate links. That means if you click one and buy something, the retailer pays us a small commission — typically a few percent of the sale. It costs you nothing extra. The price you pay is the same as if you'd gone to the store directly.

We participate in affiliate programs including Amazon Associates and retailer programs through networks like Impact, CJ Affiliate, and ShareASale (Wayfair, Target, Walmart, and specialist outdoor-furniture brands). When an article contains affiliate links, you'll see a short notice near the top of the page saying so.

Our affiliate policy (the part that matters)

This niche is built on buying once, so every affiliate link here follows three rules:

  1. Buy-it-once, never fast-furniture. We link to the durable piece that ends the replacement cycle — the teak bench, not the third steel-and-glass set. We will not recommend a product whose realistic lifespan makes its cost-per-year worse than the cheap alternative.
  2. The cost-per-year math comes first. Every recommended product carries a realistic lifespan and an annual-cost comparison. If we can't show the numbers, we don't link it.
  3. We say when the answer is "maintain what you own." Our articles regularly conclude that a cover, an oil treatment, or a re-webbed sling beats buying new. No commission is worth the trust we'd lose pretending otherwise.

We only recommend furniture we'd put on our own patios, and our guides include "what to avoid" callouts whenever we find duds. Affiliate relationships never determine our verdicts.

Display advertising

This site may display ads served through an ad network (currently set up for Mediavine Journey). These ads are how the site earns revenue from readers who never click a product link. We don't control which specific ads appear, but we choose networks with standards against deceptive or predatory advertising, and we keep ad placements from interfering with reading.

Sponsored content

If we ever publish a sponsored post or accept a sponsored newsletter placement, it will be clearly labeled as sponsored at the top of the content — and we'll only accept sponsorships for products we'd recommend unpaid. One sponsored review per quarter beats five shaky ones.

Digital products

We sell our own low-cost tools (such as a patio buying workbook and a small-patio layout pack). These are our own products, priced to be genuinely useful rather than premium courses — if a guide doesn't settle a real argument about your patio, we don't want your money for it.

Questions?

If you have questions about how we make money, or you spot something on the site that feels like it conflicts with this policy, we want to hear about it. Contact us at hello@patio-edit.com and a human will reply.
