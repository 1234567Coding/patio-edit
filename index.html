The Patio Edit is for anyone who'd rather buy patio furniture once than rebuy it every three years. Our rule is simple: every recommendation carries cost-per-year-of-ownership math — purchase price divided by realistic lifespan, in your climate — and we name the fast-furniture failure modes (chipped powder-coat, rusting steel, "teak finish" that's stained acacia) that other sites gloss over.

We don't do showroom fantasies, sponsored top-tens, or all-at-once shopping lists. The phased patio — one good piece per season — beats the all-at-once cheap set every time. If you're new, the flagship guide above is the single best place to begin.
